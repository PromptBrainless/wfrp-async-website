# Flails — Original

Quelle: Warhammer Rulebook S. 43 (https://4th.whfb.app/weapons/flails)
Status: Original, nicht anfassen

**System:** Warhammer Fantasy Battle 4. Edition.

Flails are swung with both hands and consist of heavy weights, often spiked, attached to a pole or handle by means of heavy chains. It is extremely tiring swinging these heavy and cumbersome weapons.

- Troops using flails require both arms to swing them and cannot therefore use shields in hand-to-hand fighting. If they carry shields they are put aside or slung onto backs before the fighting begins.
- In the first turn of any hand-to-hand combat troops equipped with flails add +2 to their strength. So men with a strength of 3 will hit with a strength of 5, and any enemy saves are taken with the modifier for fighting high strength opponents.
- In subsequent turns the flail users begin to tire and so they do not receive any bonus.
