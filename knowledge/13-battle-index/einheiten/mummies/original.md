## Sonderregeln

### Fear

Mummies are supernatural and disturbing monsters which cause *fear* as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Fire

Mummies are dry as tinder and many of the chemicals with which they are embalmed are highly flammable. Any Mummy hit by a flaming weapon or fiery magic spell will take double wounds, so for every wound scored the Mummy sustains 2 wounds.
