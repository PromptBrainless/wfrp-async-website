## Sonderregeln

### Daemons

All the special rules for daemons apply as described in the Warhammer rulebook. In particular, note that a stand of Nurglings has a saving  throw of 4+ due to its daemonic intangibility or daemonic aura.

### Fear

Nurglings are daemons and so cause *fear*, as described in the Warhammer rulebook. One base of Nurglings counts as one model for purposes of fear.
