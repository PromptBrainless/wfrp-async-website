## Sonderregeln

### Fear

The Gorgon causes *fear* as described in the Psychology section of the Warhammer rulebook.

### Petrify

The gaze of the Gorgon can *petrify* its opponents, turning them into stone and slaying them. The Gorgon may use its magical gaze in the magic phase. This potent spell has a range of 8" and can be used against one model that the Gorgon can see.

The target can try and avoid being turned to stone by looking aside quickly. To do this, the model must roll less than its initiative value on a D6, or else be turned to stone. On a roll of a 6 the target is always affected. even if its initiative is 6 or more.
