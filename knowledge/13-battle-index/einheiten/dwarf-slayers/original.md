## Sonderregeln

### Slayers

All Slayers are Dwarfs and the special rules already described for Dwarfs apply.

### Psychology

All Slayers are unaffected by any psychology rules except for hatred of Goblins and Orcs which affects all Dwarfs.
This means they never need take panic, fear, terror or any other psychology tests.

### Break Tests

Slayers cannot be broken from hand-to-hand combat. They do not need to take a break test if defeated in close combat and will fight to the last breath in their bodies if given the chance. As they take neither psychology or break tests their leadership is never used, but is included for comparative purposes.
