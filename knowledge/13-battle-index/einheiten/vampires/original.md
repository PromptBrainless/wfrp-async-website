## Sonderregeln

### Transfixing Glare

Vampires are able to *transfix* their victims with their glowing eyes. At the start of a hand-to-hand combat round the Vampire may attempt to transfix any model he is fighting. The model must make a successful leadership test to avoid the Vampire's glare. This is a 2D6 test, exactly like a psychology test. If this is failed the model is transfixed. A transfixed model is unable to fight that turn and any blows struck against it by the Vampire will automatically hit.
