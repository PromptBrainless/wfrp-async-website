## Sonderregeln

### Greater Daemons

All the special rules for daemons apply as described in the Warhammer rulebook. Note that the Lord of Change has a saving throw of 4+ due to its daemonic aura.

### Fly

The Lord of Change has wings and can *fly* as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Terror!

The Lord of Change is amongst the most terrifying of all monsters. The psychology rules for *terror* apply. Remember monsters which cause terror automatically cause *fear* as well.
