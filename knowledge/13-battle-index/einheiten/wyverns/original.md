## Sonderregeln

### Terror

Wyverns cause terror as described in the Psychology section of the Warhammer rulebook. Remember that a creature which causes terror automatically causes fear as well.

### Fly

The Wyvern can fly as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Scaly Skin

The Wyvern has exceptionally scaly skin which acts like armour, giving it an armour saving throw of 5 or more on a D6.
