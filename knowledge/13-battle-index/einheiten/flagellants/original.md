## Sonderregeln

### Psychology

Flagellants are not affected by panic, fear, terror, or any of the psychology rules. They are far too enwrapped by their own insane vision of imminent doom to be worried by such things.

### Combat

Flagellants will fight on in hand-to-hand combat regardless of casualties or circumstances. Flagellants need never take a break test in hand-to-hand combat. They never flee from combat, and will fight to the death even if repeatedly beaten by their opponents.

### Leadership Characteristic

As Flagellants don't take psychology or break tests they need no leadership value - in effect any test taken against their leadership is considered to have been automatically passed. Should a leadership value ever be required for comparative purposes then use the value as 10.
