## Sonderregeln

### Fear

Wights are evil and unnatural creatures which cause fear as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Immune To Psychology

Wights are not affected by psychology themselves. They are immune to fear, terror, panic and all other psychology tests.

### Break

Wights who are beaten in combat and fail their break test are destroyed. The magical hold that their master has over them is destroyed and  they crumble to dust.

### Wight Blades

Wights are armed with ancient enchanted swords, or other evilly enchanted weapons. These are magic weapons and any blow from a Wight's sword will drain the life away from its victim, causing not 1 wound on the individual but D3 (roll a D6: 1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3).
