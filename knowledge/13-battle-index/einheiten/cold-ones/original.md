## Sonderregeln

### Fear

Cold Ones are large and frightening monsters with scaly skins and hideously sharp teeth. Cold Ones and troops riding Cold Ones therefore cause *fear* as described in the Warhammer rules.

### Stupidity

Cold Ones are not very bright creatures and sometimes become confused or go inexplicably wild. They are therefore subject to *stupidity* as described in the Warhammer rulebook. This means a stupidity test must be taken at the start of each turn, using the **Ld** value of the Cold One's *rider*.
