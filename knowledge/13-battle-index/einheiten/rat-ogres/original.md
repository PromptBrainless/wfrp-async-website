## Sonderregeln

### Fear

Rat-Ogres are big, frightening blasphemies against nature which cause *fear* in other creatures. The psychological rules for fear therefore apply.

### Stupidity

Being incredibly single-minded, Rat-Ogres that aren't in close combat are subject to the psychological rules for *stupidity*. However, as long as they are led by a Clan Moulder Packmaster or a Skaven character acting as Packmaster the Rat-Ogres don't have to think for themselves and don't suffer from stupidity.
