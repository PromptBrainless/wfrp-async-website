## Sonderregeln

### Terror

Chimeras are extremely large and horrifying monsters and cause *terror* as described in the Warhammer rulebook. Remember that creatures which cause terror automatically cause *fear* too.

### Breathe Fire

The Chimera's dragon head can *breathe fire* in the shooting phase. Use the teardrop-shaped flame template to work out hits. Place the template with the end lying over the target and the narrow part coming from the chimera's mouth. Any model lying under the template area is hit on a D6 score of a 4 or more. Fire hits have a strength of 4.
