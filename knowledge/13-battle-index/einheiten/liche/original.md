## Sonderregeln

### Fear

A Liche is a corpse-like creature which causes fear. Liches are covered by the psychology rules for fear as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Immune To Psychology

A Liche is not affected by psychology. He is immune to fear, terror, panic and all other psychology tests.
