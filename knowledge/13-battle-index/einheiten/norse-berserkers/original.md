## Sonderregeln

### Frenzy

Berserkers are affected by the rules for *frenzy* described in the Warhammer rulebook. This means that they will always *charge* enemy within reach, being so enraged they have no choice but to rush forward and throw themselves upon their foes. As with all frenzied troops Berserkers double their attacks and will therefore have 2 attacks instead of 1. Also, frenzied warriors always pursue enemy who break and flee in combat. See the description of *frenzy* for full details of how this applies.
