## Sonderregeln

### Move

Spiders have eight hairy legs which enable them to move easily over obstacles and rough territory. In woodlands they can swing from tree to tree or scramble over the treetops to drop to the forest floor below. To represent this spiders are not slowed down by crossing difficult ground or obstacles.
