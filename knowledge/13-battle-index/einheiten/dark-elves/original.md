## Sonderregeln

### Hate High Elves

Dark Elves hate their ancient enemies the High Elves so intensely that they will often fight them to the death rather than run away. When fighting High Elves, Dark Elves are affected by the rules for *hatred* as described in the Warhammer rulebook.
