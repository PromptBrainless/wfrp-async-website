## Sonderregeln

### Daemons

All the special rules for daemons apply as described in the Warhammer rulebook. In particular, note that the Juggernaut has a saving throw of 4+ due to its daemonic intangibility or daemonic aura.

### Fear

Juggernauts are horrendous and powerful creatures. A Juggernaut rider therefore causes *fear* as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Crush

Juggernauts have 2 attacks: one conventional attack to represent butting and goring and one *crush* attack to represent their trampling hooves. A hit from a crush attack automatically causes 1 wound - the target's toughness and armour give it no protection.
