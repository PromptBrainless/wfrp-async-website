## Sonderregeln

### Daemons

All the special rules for daemons apply as described in the Warhammer rulebook. In particular note that the Bloodletter has a saving throw of 4+ due to its daemonic intangibility or daemonic aura.

### Fear

As monstrous and horrifying creatures the Bloodletters cause fear, as described in the Warhammer rulebook.

### Hellblade

The Hellblade drips continually with blood and is enriched with enchantment. A hit from a Hellblade causes not one wound on its victim, as most hits do, but D3 wounds (roll a D6: 1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3).
