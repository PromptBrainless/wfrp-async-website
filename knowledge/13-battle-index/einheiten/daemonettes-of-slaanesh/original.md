### Daemons

All the special rules for daemons apply as described in the Warhammer rulebook. In particular note that the Daemonette has a saving throw of 4+ due to its daemonic intangibility or daemonic aura.

As monstrous and uncanny creatures the Daemonettes cause *fear*, as described in the Psychology rules.
